# 布光模块（三套可复用 + 8 维度拆解法）

> 【布光锁定】是整条片子的**视觉指纹**。它必须全片逐字一致 —— 不同任务各写一套，
> 跨段就会像两部片子拼的。
>
> 下面三套是实战验证过的现成模块，换项目时只改 3 处（调色五项 / 声源清单 / 运动方式）。

---

## 模块 A · 日系青春（来源：用户提供的 4 段短剧范本）

```
【布光锁定】Shot in the style of late-1990s Japanese youth cinema, Iwai Shunji aesthetic.
Hybrid texture: 35mm film grain blended with low-resolution MiniDV video noise, soft focus,
heavy diffusion, dreamy hazy bloom around all highlights. Faded low-saturation palette —
pale jade green, milky white, dust blue, bleached straw yellow, weak skin tones.
Overexposed daylight, blown-out white sky, natural light only, no fill, veiling lens flare.
Handheld camera, operator body sway, micro-jitter, imperfect framing, auto-focus hunting,
auto-exposure pulsing. Poetic realism, documentary intimacy, loneliness and freedom,
nostalgic summer memory. Ambient sound only: cicadas, wind, distant traffic, tape hiss.
```

**特征**：胶片噪点 + MiniDV 双重质感、过曝、手持晃动、对焦拉风箱。
**适用**：青春片、怀旧、校园、独立短片。
**不要用于**：任何需要「新、净、贵」的商业项目 —— 噪点和晃动会直接读成廉价。

---

## 模块 B · 建发高端（高净值地产 / 会所）

```
【布光锁定】Shot in the style of contemporary high-end residential lifestyle film.
Clean digital cinema texture with subtle filmic depth, 35mm full-frame, medium shallow depth of field,
no soft focus, no diffusion bloom, no grain. Warm champagne and ivory palette with soft jade-blue
accents, deep charcoal stone, muted brass reflection; skin tones kept natural and warm, not bleached.
Interior available light only: recessed linear ceiling coves as the key, warm daylight through
floor-to-ceiling glazing as the fill, soft bounced lift from stone and brass surfaces,
no overexposure, highlights fully retained. Layered practical lighting: warm cove light rakes across
ceiling and wall, daylight wraps the subject from the window side, polished stone and lacquered brass
carry controlled specular highlights, shadows fall off gradually into soft depth, never flat even lighting.
Locked-off camera and extremely slow dolly moves only; no handheld shake, no zoom, no whip pan,
no lens flare, no vignette. Calm, lived-in, quietly premium; multi-generational leisure,
unhurried weekend ease, the feeling of a private resort one elevator ride away.
Ambient sound only: water lapping, ball-pit rustle, table-tennis knock, pages turning,
distant laughter, soft footsteps.
```

**特征**：干净数码质感、无颗粒、分层实用光（灯槽斜擦 + 落地窗包裹 + 石材黄铜受控高光）、阴影渐隐、锁死机位。
**适用**：高端住宅、会所、酒店、商业空间广告。

## 模块 C · 温馨日常（推荐用于「一家人」的生活叙事）

```
【布光锁定】Warm, lived-in family lifestyle film. Soft natural daylight as the only key,
gentle contrast, no dramatic lighting, no moody shadows. Warm honey and cream palette with
soft sage and dusty blue accents, light oak wood, linen and cotton texture; skin tones healthy,
warm and natural. Daylight comes through windows and softly bounces off pale walls;
shadow edges stay soft and never crushed; a slight morning haze keeps the air clean.
Everything looks like a real home a family actually lives in — comfortable, unstyled, quietly warm.
Camera stays steady and unobtrusive; no handheld shake, no zoom, no lens flare, no vignette.
Ambient sound only: water, kettle, footsteps, pages turning, distant laughter.
```

**特征**：柔光、低对比、蜜糖奶油底色、亚麻棉布质感、无戏剧光、无暗调。
**适用**：家庭生活、亲情叙事、生活方式品牌。
**与模块 B 的区别**：B 是「高级」，C 是「温暖」；B 强调材质反射与精密光，C 强调柔与生活感。

## 8 维度拆解法（自己写新模块时照这个顺序）

范本的力量在于**每个维度都是具体名词/动词，不是形容词**：

| # | 维度 | 写什么 | 反例 → 正例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **影调坐标** | 年代 / 流派 / 导演参考 | 「电影感」→ `late-1990s Japanese youth cinema, Iwai Shunji aesthetic` |
| 2 | **质感** | 介质组合（颗粒/噪点/数码） | 「胶片感」→ `35mm film grain blended with MiniDV video noise` |
| 3 | **光学** | 柔焦 / 扩散 / 光晕 / 景深 | 「柔光」→ `soft focus, heavy diffusion, hazy bloom, shallow DOF on a fast prime` |
| 4 | **调色** | **3–5 个具名颜色** | 「低饱和」→ `pale jade green, milky white, dust blue, bleached straw yellow` |
| 5 | **曝光** | 过曝/保留高光/补光/阴影 | 「自然光」→ `overexposed, natural light only, no fill, highlights fully retained` |
| 6 | **运动** | 手持/稳定/速度/瑕疵 | 「稳定」→ `locked-off camera, extremely slow dolly, no handheld, no zoom` |
| 7 | **情绪** | 学派 + 感受 | 「温馨」→ `calm, lived-in, quietly premium, unhurried weekend ease` |
| 8 | **声源** | **具体声源清单**（不是「环境声」） | 「环境声」→ `water lapping, pages turning, distant laughter` |

**调色五项、声源清单、运动方式** —— 这三项是换项目时**必须改**的，其余七项（质感/光学/曝光/情绪/影调）可以沿用。

---

## 可迁移的四个电影感来源（不靠形容词）

1. `shallow depth of field` + `natural bokeh` —— 浅景深与散景
2. `backlit through sheer curtains` —— 纱帘逆光（比正面打光高级得多）
3. `highlights roll off softly` + `deep but clean shadows` —— 高光柔和滚降、暗部保留细节（不是死黑）
4. `no flat even lighting` —— 明确禁止平光（平光 = 廉价）

**前景虚化要克制**：`soft foreground occlusion` 用多了会变成「偷窥视角」。
真正需要它的只有四种情形：建立旁观视角、框式转场、遮挡次要信息、制造纵深。
不要每段都用 —— 全片 8 段用 5 次已经是上限。
